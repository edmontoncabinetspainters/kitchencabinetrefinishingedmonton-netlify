How to Prepare Edmonton Kitchen Cabinets for Refinishing – A Step-by-Step Guide

Published on March 15, 2024 | By Royal Painting Team

Proper preparation is crucial for achieving a professional and long-lasting cabinet refinishing result. In this comprehensive guide, we'll walk you through the essential steps to prepare your kitchen cabinets for refinishing, ensuring the best possible outcome for your kitchen transformation.

1. Clear Out and Clean Up

Before any refinishing work begins, you'll need to:

  • Remove all items from your cabinets and drawers
  • Clear countertops and nearby surfaces
  • Clean cabinet surfaces thoroughly with a degreaser to remove cooking oils and residue
  • Allow surfaces to dry completely

2. Document and Label

Organization is key to a smooth refinishing process:

  • Take photos of your cabinet layout for reference
  • Number or label each door and drawer
  • Create a diagram showing where each piece belongs
  • Store hardware (hinges, handles, screws) in labeled bags

3. Remove Hardware and Doors

Careful removal ensures no damage to your cabinets:

  • Remove all handles, knobs, and pulls
  • Unscrew hinges from doors and cabinet frames
  • Place doors and drawers in a designated work area
  • Keep track of all hardware and their locations

4. Repair and Prepare Surfaces

Address any existing damage before refinishing:

  • Fill holes, dents, and scratches with wood filler
  • Sand down rough spots and repair edges
  • Check for loose joints or damaged areas
  • Replace any severely damaged pieces

5. Create a Dust-Free Work Environment

Protect your kitchen during the refinishing process:

  • Cover countertops, appliances, and floors with drop cloths
  • Seal off the kitchen from other rooms with plastic sheeting
  • Ensure proper ventilation for safety
  • Set up adequate lighting for detailed work
